About CaptureOsa

Founded in 2015 by award-winning wildlife photographer James Martinez, CaptureOsa began with a vision to share the incredible biodiversity of Costa Rica's Osa Peninsula with photographers worldwide. What started as small group workshops has grown into a renowned photography education program, helping hundreds of photographers develop their skills while promoting wildlife conservation.

Our mission is to inspire and educate photographers while fostering a deep respect for nature and wildlife. We believe in responsible photography practices that prioritize animal welfare and environmental conservation. Through our workshops, we aim to create advocates for Costa Rica's unique ecosystems while helping photographers capture their beauty in extraordinary ways.

Frequently Asked Questions

What skill level is required?

Our workshops accommodate all skill levels, from complete beginners to seasoned professionals. We tailor our instruction to meet each participant's needs and ensure everyone grows significantly during their time with us.

What equipment should I bring?

We recommend a DSLR or mirrorless camera, telephoto lens (minimum 300mm), wide-angle lens, macro lens (optional), tripod, and laptop with Adobe Lightroom installed. A detailed equipment list will be provided upon booking.

What's included in the workshop price?

Workshop fees cover accommodation, local transportation, meals, photography instruction, post-processing sessions, and park entrance fees. International flights and equipment are not included.

How physically demanding are the workshops?

Workshops involve moderate hiking on established trails. Participants should be comfortable walking 3-4 miles per day with camera gear. We can accommodate different fitness levels and adjust activities accordingly.

What's the best time to visit?

While Costa Rica offers great photography opportunities year-round, December to April (dry season) typically provides the most stable weather conditions and highest wildlife activity.
